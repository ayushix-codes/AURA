# AURA

### The AI Layer for Personalized Beauty & Style

> **Your Vision. Your Look. Your AURA.**

AURA is an AI-powered beauty and style platform that helps users turn beauty inspiration and personal preferences into personalized looks, visualizations, professional recommendations, service plans, and bookings.

Instead of simply finding a salon or choosing a service, AURA connects the entire journey from **what you imagine** to **getting the look created in real life**.

---

## What AURA Does

AURA brings together AI, beauty discovery, professional portfolios, personalization, community, and booking in one platform.

### AI Beauty & Style Studio

Users can describe the look they want and build a complete personalized look using:

- Makeup
- Hairstyles
- Nails
- Skincare and facials
- Spa and wellness
- Grooming
- Jewelry
- Outfit
- Occasion
- Personal style

Users can also describe their desired look naturally instead of selecting everything manually.

Example:

> "I want soft glam with a little glitter, neutral eyes that still pop, and a hairstyle that works with my outfit."

AURA can interpret the request and use it to create a personalized beauty direction.

## Professional Bookmarks

Users can bookmark professionals they want to revisit later.

Bookmarks allow users to:

- Save favorite professionals
- Revisit profiles
- Compare professionals
- View portfolios later
- Book when ready

### AURA Points and Bookmarks Are Independent

AURA Points and professional bookmarks represent different actions.

Giving AURA Points does not automatically bookmark a professional.

Removing a bookmark does not remove previously given AURA Points.

---

## AURA Points

AURA Points are a preference signal for professionals.

They are separate from traditional ratings and reviews.

Example:

**4.9 Rating | 1,240 AURA Points**

Users can give AURA Points to professionals whose work they appreciate or prefer.

## Service-Based Booking

AURA connects personalized looks with real beauty services.

A complete look may require multiple professionals.

For example:

- Facial → Professional A
- Hair → Professional B
- Makeup → Professional C
- Nails → Professional D

### One Look → Multiple Specialists → One Plan

Future multi-professional scheduling can consider:

- Availability
- Duration
- Location
- Travel distance
- Scheduling conflicts
- Specialization
- Price

The initial free experience can support up to 3 appointments, with expanded planning planned for premium users.

AURA can also support professionals offering home services.
